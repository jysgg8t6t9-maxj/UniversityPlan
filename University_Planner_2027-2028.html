How This Planner Works

❖

This planner follows the rhythm of your academic year — from the first Monday of term to the close of exam season. Each section is colour–tabbed along the edge of the page, so if you print and bind it, you can find your way in by colour alone.

Print double–sided on A5, or two–up on A4 and trim, then bind with a disc system, ring binder, or simply have it stapled at a print shop. The coloured tabs are designed to peek out from the edge once bound.

Looking After Yourself

❖

Good terms are built on good basics. This section is small on purpose — just enough structure to notice patterns, never enough to feel like one more thing to perform.

A Self-Care Menu

Step outside, even for ten minutes
Message a friend — not about work
Cook something properly, slowly
Move your body, however that looks today
Write down three things, good or bad
Go to bed before you mean to
Ask for help before you need it
